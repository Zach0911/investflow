# Company Research

## Overview

Build a structured company research summary before forming a thesis or valuation view.

## Required Inputs

- Company or ticker
- Market
- Research horizon
- Data sources and timestamp
- Whether the output is for learning, watchlist, holding review, or decision support

## Current Data Rule

Use up-to-date filings, earnings releases, investor materials, market data, and credible news when claims depend on current facts. If current data is unavailable, mark the analysis as framework-only.

## Workflow

1. Describe business model and revenue structure.
2. Identify key products, customers, geographies, and growth drivers.
3. Review financial quality: revenue, margin, cash flow, debt, dilution, and capital allocation.
4. Compare competitive position and industry structure.
5. Check governance, regulatory, and event risks.
6. End with open questions that must be answered before a thesis.

## Output Format

```markdown
## 公司研究摘要

## 商业模式

## 财务质量

## 竞争格局

## 核心优势

## 核心风险

## 待验证问题
```

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing a good company with a good investment.
- Summarizing news without financial quality.
- Ignoring balance sheet or dilution risk.
