# Postmortem

## Overview

Review the quality of the decision process separately from the investment outcome.

## Required Inputs

- Instrument
- Original decision and date
- Original thesis
- Position size
- Entry and exit or current status
- Actual result
- New information that emerged

## Workflow

1. Reconstruct the original decision from contemporaneous facts.
2. Separate outcome from process quality.
3. Identify whether the thesis was right, wrong, incomplete, or untested.
4. Attribute mistakes to information, analysis, emotion, execution, sizing, or external shock.
5. Extract one or two process changes for future decisions.

## Output Format

```markdown
## 原始决策

## 实际结果

## Thesis 复盘

## 错误来源

## 可复用经验

## 下次行动规则
```

## Common Mistakes

- Calling a profitable outcome a good decision without checking process.
- Treating every loss as a mistake.
- Adding too many future rules that the user will not follow.
