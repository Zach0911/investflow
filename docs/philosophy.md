# Philosophy

InvestFlow treats investing as a research and decision process, not a prediction contest.

The project assumes that AI agents are most useful when they improve discipline:

- They slow the user down before emotional decisions.
- They separate facts from assumptions.
- They make missing information visible.
- They force a risk review before action.
- They turn outcomes into reusable lessons.

The goal is not to make AI more confident. The goal is to make AI more accountable.
