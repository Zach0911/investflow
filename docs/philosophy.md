# Philosophy

InvestFlow treats investing as a research and decision process, not a prediction contest.

The project assumes that AI agents are most useful when they improve discipline:

- They slow the user down before emotional decisions.
- They separate facts from assumptions.
- They make missing information visible.
- They force a risk review before action.
- They turn outcomes into reusable lessons.

The goal is not to make AI more confident. The goal is to make AI more accountable.

## Operating Beliefs

- A vague investment question should become a research task brief before it becomes a conclusion.
- A research task should become an execution plan before it becomes a report.
- A conclusion should pass a decision gate before it becomes an actionable label.
- A report should carry data sources, timestamps, invalidation conditions, position boundaries, and review triggers.
- A past decision should become a postmortem loop, not just a memory.

## Product Direction

InvestFlow is intentionally closer to agent workflow infrastructure than to a stock-picking product. The project should optimize for repeatable research behavior, transparent evidence, and reviewable outputs.

The project should not optimize for speed at the cost of missing context, confidence without evidence, or convenience that blurs the line into trade execution.
